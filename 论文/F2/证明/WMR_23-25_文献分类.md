# WMR_23-25 文献分类

> 来源文件 `WMR_23-25.pdf` 是某稿件的参考文献表（第 23–25 页，条目 F–Z），**只有题名与出处，没有摘要**，
> 因此下列分类依据题名判断。A–E 段（第 21–22 页）不在该文件内。
> 两组均按与本文的相关度从高到低排列。

## 一、F2 / flow shop 问题

- **Shabtay & Gerstl (2024)** — Coordinating scheduling and rejection decisions in a two-machine flow shop scheduling problem, *EJOR*. 该表中唯一直接的两机器 flow shop。
- **Panwalkar & Koulamas (2015)** — On equivalence between the proportionate flow shop and single-machine scheduling problems, *NRL*. 结构性等价，可迁移到本文的 block 论证。
- **Halman (2025)** — A faster FPTAS for proportionate flow shop scheduling with step-deteriorating processing times, *J. Sched.*. 与本文 FPTAS 路线一致。
- **Shabtay & Mor (2024)** — Exact algorithms and approximation schemes for proportionate flow shop scheduling with step-deteriorating processing times, *J. Sched.*.
- **Panwalkar, Smith & Koulamas (2013)** — Review of the ordered and proportionate flow shop scheduling research, *NRL*. 综述。
- **Shabtay & Oron (2016)** — Proportionate flow-shop scheduling with rejection, *JORS*.
- **Li, Qian & Chen (2017)** — Proportionate flow shop scheduling with rejection, *APJOR*.
- **Gerstl, Mor & Mosheiov (2019)** — Scheduling on a proportionate flowshop to minimise total late work, *IJPR*.
- **Koulamas (2020)** — The proportionate flow shop total tardiness problem, *EJOR*.
- **Mor, Mosheiov & Shabtay (2021)** — Minimizing the total tardiness and job rejection cost in a proportionate flow shop with generalized due dates, *J. Sched.*.
- **Qian & Han (2022)** — Improved algorithms for proportionate flow shop scheduling with due-window assignment, *AOR*.
- **Geng, Sun, Wang & Pan (2023)** — Scheduling on proportionate flow shop with job rejection and common due date assignment, *CIE*.
- **Geng, Sun, Wang & Mor (2025)** — Scheduling on proportionate flowshop with total late work and job rejection, *Operational Research*.
- **Mor (2026)** — Maximizing the weighted number of Just-In-Time jobs in a proportionate flow shop with job rejection, *Operational Research*.
- **Shabtay (2026)** — Coordinating scheduling and resource allocation decisions in a proportionate flow-shop scheduling environment, *Optimization Letters*.
- **Wang, Lv & Wan (2025)** — Proportionate flow shop scheduling with job-dependent due windows and position-dependent weights, *APJOR*.
- **Ying, Pourhejazy & Sung (2024)** — Two-agent proportionate flowshop scheduling with deadlines, *AOR*.
- **Yu & Han (2021)** — Scheduling proportionate flow shops with preventive machine maintenance, *IJPE*.
- **Xiong, Jing, Xiang & Yitong (2025)** — Order acceptance and scheduling under eligibility, availability, and budget constraints in distributed heterogeneous flow shop production, *ESWA*.

## 二、以 WCmax（加权完成时间 / 加权 makespan）为目标

- **Shakhlevich, Hoogeveen & Pinedo (1998)** — Minimizing total weighted completion time in a proportionate flow shop, *J. Sched.*. 目标函数与本文最接近，同时属 flow shop（与第一类交叉）。
- **Li (2015)** — Best-possible online algorithms for single machine scheduling to minimize the maximum weighted completion time, *APJOR*.
- **Lu, Zuo, Zhang & Ou (2025)** — Order acceptance and scheduling with weighted makespan, *4OR*.
- **Sun (2024)** — Randomized approximation schemes for minimizing the weighted makespan on identical parallel machines, *J. Comb. Optim.*.
- **Zuo, Zhang, Shi & Fan (2026)** — Single machine scheduling with a restricted rate-modifying activity to minimize the weighted makespan, *DAM*.
